title: 外滩 哪能 走 ？ | How do I get to the Bund?
setting: Sunday afternoon. Sam wants to see the Bund and stops a man at a bus stop.
--- lines
Sam: 爷叔 ， 请问 一声 ， 外滩 哪能 走 ？ | Excuse me, sir, how do I get to the Bund? | uncle, may-I-ask one-word, Bund how go?
爷叔: 外滩 啊 ？ 蛮 远 个 。 侬 坐 地铁 好 了 。 | The Bund? It's quite far. Just take the metro. | Bund (eh)? quite far (it is). you sit metro (and that's it)
Sam: 地铁站 辣 啥地方 ？ | Where's the metro station? | metro-station at what-place?
爷叔: 就 辣 前头 ， 一直[iɪʔ55_zəʔ12] 跑 ， 五 分钟 。 | Just ahead. Walk straight on, five minutes. | right at front, straight walk, five minutes
Sam: 要 坐 几 站 ？ | How many stops? | need sit how-many stop?
爷叔: 三 站 ， 南京东路[nø13_ʨin53_toŋ53_lu13] 落车[loʔ12_ʦʰo53] 。 | Three stops. Get off at East Nanjing Road.
Sam: 谢谢 爷叔 ！ | Thank you, sir!
爷叔: 勿客气 。 白相 开心 ！ | You're welcome. Have fun! | not-polite. play happy!
--- notes
1: 请问一声 "may I ask a word" is the polite way to stop someone in the street. 哪能 na-nen is "how" (Mandarin 怎么): 哪能走 "how (do I) go".
2: 好了 at the end of a suggestion means "just do that": 侬坐地铁好了 "just take the metro". 坐 is "to sit", and also "to take" a bus or the metro.
3: 辣 laq + a place is "at, in" (Mandarin 在): 辣啥地方 "at what place = where". 啥地方 is the everyday "where"; 哪里 also exists.
4: 跑 is a false friend: in Shanghainese it is "to walk, to go on foot". To run is 奔. 前头 is "in front, ahead".
6: 落车 "get off" (Mandarin 下车) uses 落 "to come down". In this word 车 is said tsho, its everyday reading; you will also hear the reading ciu in book words.
8: 白相 is "to have fun, to play, to go out": one of the most Shanghainese words there is. 开心 is "happy".
