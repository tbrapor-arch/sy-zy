# Card: what reviews have found (one line each; read before writing, add after each review)

## Readings
- A word's reading is not the sum of its characters: 文读 in learned words (问题 vən, 大学 dᴀ, 希望 ɦuɑ̃), 白读 in
  everyday ones (物事 məʔ, 耳环 guᴇ). Never accept a composed reading unchecked: lint refuses it from lesson 50.
- 弄 "to do" is noŋ53; 弄堂 keeps loŋ13. 合算 starts with k. The question 阿 (阿是, 阿要) is ᴀʔ, short.
- One character, two words: 只 ʦᴀʔ (measure) / ʦəʔ (only); 着 ʦᴀʔ (wear) / zᴀʔ (result). Force the reading: 字[IPA].

## Grammar
- Completion is 了 in today's Shanghainese. 仔 is the older form (older speakers, novels): taught as such at L66.
- A state that lasts is verb + 辣海 (摆辣海, 坐辣海); in progress is 辣辣 + verb. Bring 辣海 back often: it was missing
  from lessons 1–49.
- "Take X and do" is 拿, not 把. No 被: the passive is 拨.

## Words
- Prefer the Shanghainese word the course already has: 楼高头 (not 楼上), 爷叔 (not 叔叔), 揿 (not 按), 阔 (not 宽),
  力道 (not 力气), 生毛病 (not 生病), 姆妈 (not 妈妈).
- One written form per word: 伐 (not 哦 / 𠲎), 介末 (not 葛末), 搿 (not 迭), 困 (not 睏), 㑚 (not 倷). Other spellings
  appear only where a lesson teaches them (L50, L100).

## Notes
- Never type a romanization: `{伐}` or `{只[ʦᴀʔ55]}` in a note shows the spelling in the learner's system.
- A note that names a reading "everyday" or "literary" must be checked against a dictionary.

## Readings (review of 50–63)
- A reading goes into `core.tsv` only after it is checked against a printed source, tone class included: four
  "corrections" made from memory in 1–49 were wrong (看见, 错, 又, 美国) and were undone here.
- A book-confirmed reading that differs from the lexicon goes into `overrides.tsv` with the lesson cited (阿里 ɦᴀ,
  勿错 and 搓 in -o, 呢 nəʔ).
- A number used in an exercise must have been met in a dialogue first.
- Check the printed course for everyday verbs too, not only new words: 乘 is ʦʰən (printed cen, 16 times), not the
  lexicon's zən; 恐怕 has 怕 in -o; 塞车 has a checked 塞; 脚踏车 has 车 ʦʰo (review of 64–77).
- 阿 in kin terms (阿哥, 阿弟, 阿妹, 阿伯, 阿娘) is the checked syllable ᴀʔ55, as in 阿姐 and 阿拉: the lexicon's ᴀ53 is wrong
  for them.
- Write 每日天 for "every day" (not 天天), 日[ȵiɪʔ12] for a day counted (一日, 两日), 着 for wearing clothes.
