# Course content

Audience: English-speaking learners who read Chinese and speak Mandarin at around B2. Words written with the same
characters as Mandarin are learned as pronunciation; teaching time goes to false friends, Shanghainese-only words,
文白 readings, tone sandhi and grammar words. Variety: new urban Shanghainese, 5 tones.

## Shape (as in Tsheh-tû)
- 100 lessons, a review every 7th lesson, second wave from lesson 50.
- Progression L1–50 by number of lines (`tools/lint.py`, `docs/course-plan.md` §3): 6 lines at L1–5, one more every
  5 lessons (15 at L46–50); a median line of 7 syllables at L1–10, rising by 1 every 10 lessons; 12 → 20 new words;
  review lessons 2 lines shorter, no new words. Exercises: 3 translate + 3 fill + 3 say, new sentences (never a dialogue line), words already met only.

## Rules for content
1. Everything here is original. No sentences, dialogues or notes from published courses or from the books in the
   private repository: those books only confirm readings and inform word choice.
2. Each word is stored once, in IPA (`data/raw/zaonhe.txt`, corrected by `core.tsv` and `overrides.tsv`); Wugniu,
   Qian Nairong pinyin and IPA are generated by `tools/romanize.py`. Never type a romanization into a lesson.
3. Characters: simplified (Qian Nairong's choices for dialect words); traditional is generated by the app.

## Lesson file (`lessons/NNN.yaml`)
```yaml
id: 1
title: {en: Good morning!, hanzi: 侬早 ！}
review: false                 # true every 7th lesson
setting: >-                   # one or two sentences, in English
  A lane in Jing'an, nine in the morning.
dialogue:
- n: 1
  speaker: 阿姨
  hanzi: 侬 是 新[ɕin53] 来 个 伐 ？   # words separated by spaces = sandhi domains; 字[IPA_IPA] forces a reading
  en: You're new here, aren't you?
  lit: you are newly come one, (question)?   # optional word-for-word
notes:
- ref: 3                      # line number
  topic: grammar              # optional: vocab, grammar, pronunciation, culture
  text: >
    伐 at the end turns a statement into a yes–no question…
exercises:
  translate: [{hanzi: …, en: …}]          # read it, give the English (3)
  fill: [{hanzi: 我 ＿ Sam 。, en: …, answer: 叫}]   # (3)
  say: [{hanzi: …, en: …}]                # English → Shanghainese (3)
# Exercise sentences are new (lint fails on a copy of any dialogue line) and use only words met so far.
```
Lesson 0 (how to read the spellings) is `lessons/000-reading.html`.

## Word files
- `core.tsv`: ~230 core words written by hand (type, English, Mandarin, corrected IPA). Check the flagged ones first.
- `overrides.tsv`: corrections confirmed against a book: they win over everything else.
- `verified.tsv`, `book_notes.tsv`: written by `tools/import_notes.py` from the app's "book notes" file.
