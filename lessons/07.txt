title: 复习 | Review
review: yes
setting: Sunday evening. Sam is back in the lane; the auntie is sitting outside her door.
--- lines
阿姨: Sam ， 侬 今朝 做 啥 了 ？ | Sam, what did you do today? | Sam, you today do what (now)?
Sam: 我 搭 小李[ɕiɔ34_li13] 一道 去 豫园[ɦy13_ɦyø13] 白相 了 。 | I went to Yuyuan with Xiao Li. | I and Little-Li together go Yuyuan play (done)
阿姨: 远 伐 ？ | Was it far?
Sam: 勿 远 ， 阿拉 坐 地铁 去 个 。 | Not far, we took the metro. | not far, we sit metro go (it was)
阿姨: 小笼 吃 过 了 伐 ？ | Did you have soup dumplings?
Sam: 吃 过 了 ， 老 好吃 个 ！ 就是[ʑiɤ13_zɿ13] 忒 烫 了 。 | Yes, really good! Just too hot. | eaten (done), very tasty (really)! only-that too hot (now)
阿姨: 侬 上海闲话 越[ɦyɪʔ12] 讲 越[ɦyɪʔ12] 好 了 ！ | Your Shanghainese gets better and better! | you Shanghai-talk more speak more good (now)
Sam: 谢谢 阿姨 ！ 侬 是 我 个 老师 。 | Thanks, auntie! You're my teacher.
--- notes
1: Questions, so far. A yes–no question ends in 伐: 远伐？ 吃过了伐？ A question with a question word needs no 伐: 啥 what, 啥人 who, 啥地方 where, 几钿 how much, 几 how many, 哪能 how, 做啥 why.
2: Pronouns: 我 I, 侬 you, 伊 he or she; 阿拉 we, 㑚 you (plural), 伊拉 they. 阿拉 is also "my" for family and home: 阿拉屋里, 阿拉老公.
4: Three ways to say no. 勿 veq "not": 勿远, 勿是. 覅 viau "don't!": 覅客气. 呒没 m-meq "there isn't, haven't": 还呒没 "not yet".
4: 个 at the end of a sentence (去个, 好吃个) confirms or stresses: "that's how it was", "it really is". The same 个 after a noun marks possession: 我个老师 "my teacher".
6: 了 at the end of a sentence shows a change or something done: 吃过了 "eaten", 忒烫了 "(it got) too hot", 好了 "better now".
7: 越…越… "the more…the more…": 越讲越好 "the more you speak, the better (it gets)", as in Mandarin.
