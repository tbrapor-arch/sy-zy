# Cast and story (read before writing a lesson; add two lines per lesson written)

## Cast
- **Sam**: French, works in an office in Shanghai (new job from L51). Lives in a lane (弄堂) in Jing'an since L1; his
  lease was ending at L12, when he visited a flat in an old lane house. Sister in Paris (L32); his parents visit at L93. Learner: his
  Shanghainese grows with the course, and people still praise or correct it.
- **阿姨** (the auntie next door): retired, born in the lane, the course's main voice of old Shanghai. Calls him Sam; he
  calls her 阿姨. Cooks (L18, L38, L45, L49), knows every stallholder (L25), has opinions on the neighbours (L22, L33).
  Her son works in New York (L29); her grandson (外孙, eight at L20) appears again at L50 and L61; her brother at L96.
- **小李**: colleague and closest friend, grew up in Hongkou. Sam calls him 小李. His parents (小李姆妈, 小李爷) had Sam to
  dinner at L31 and come back at L53 (father's broken leg), L57 (mahjong), L78 (mother teaches red-braised pork),
  L89 (father's smoking). His grandmother: Suzhou at L36, Songjiang at L76 — keep to one story when writing L76.
- **小张**: met at the language exchange (L39), a designer looking for work (L55). Her boyfriend **阿强** (L48: they had
  quarrelled at L41 and made up). She moves flat (L64), gets an offer to study abroad (L90) and leaves at L97.
- **Neighbours and passers-by**: 爷叔 at the breakfast stall (L2); 阿婆, the old neighbour on her stool (L26, L33); the
  family upstairs (L22: their cat at L67, the leak at L80); the repairman 师傅 (L37, L80, L94); 老王, the retired
  neighbour of the narrated L85. Service roles are named by role: 师傅, 营业员, 服务员, 医生, 房东, 摊主, 司机.

## How they speak
- The auntie: the most local speech, final particles, sayings; she is the one who explains old words (L44, L73).
- Xiao Li: young urban Shanghainese, plain and quick. Xiao Zhang: careful, a little bookish. Sam: short sentences
  early on, longer ones from L50, still makes the occasional learner's slip that someone corrects.
- Address: 阿姨 / 爷叔 / 阿婆 for older people, 师傅 for drivers and tradesmen, 小 + surname among colleagues and friends.

## Story so far (lessons 1–49)
Sam arrives in the lane (1–7), finds his feet at work and in the neighbourhood (8–21), settles: a new flat, a bank
account, the market with the auntie (22–28), is drawn into two families: the auntie's and Xiao Li's (29–35), travels
and makes new friends: Suzhou, Xiao Zhang, the French Concession (36–42), and handles daily troubles in
Shanghainese: a broken phone, a queue-jumper, a couple's quarrel (43–49).

## Ahead (lessons 50–100, from course/syllabus.yaml)
50–55 second wave: Sam writes in Shanghainese, changes jobs. 57–69 the friends' lives: a wedding, sport, a move, the
cat, breakfast. 71–83 travel, the past, family names, money, trouble at home. 85–100 narrated passages take over:
Old Wang's day, plum rain, the lane's people, Pudong; Xiao Zhang leaves (97); Sam tells his own story (99) and opens
his first Shanghainese novel (100).

## Lessons written from 50 on (two lines each: what happened; what to remember)
- **50** Sam posts in the lane's WeChat group in Shanghainese; the grandson asks about 哦 / 葛末 and 大学 / 大小.
  Remember: the grandson calls Sam 爷叔; spellings and 文读 / 白读 are now explained, the course keeps 伐 and 介末.
- **51** Sam's day in the new job, told to the auntie: up at six, an hour of metro, overtime, 泡饭 for breakfast.
  Remember: the new office is far and keeps him late; he sometimes cannot sleep.
- **52** The auntie's glasses are lost; they search the flat and find them on her head.
  Remember: the glasses are a running joke (back at L56); position words and 辣海 for where things lie.
- **53** Xiao Li's father slipped in the lane on a rainy day and broke his leg: Sam visits him in a Hongkou hospital.
  Remember: a month lying down, hates his syrup; Sam calls him 爷叔; a nurse (护士) speaks.
- **54** A typhoon in August: the auntie has Sam bring in the washing, shut the windows and block the door.
  Remember: the old house lets water in under the door; power cuts were common in her youth.
- **55** Xiao Zhang rehearses a job interview at a design company with Sam playing the manager.
  Remember: she graduated in design two years ago; she is nervous but good.
- **56** (review) The morning after the typhoon. Remember: the father goes home soon; Xiao Zhang got the job and
  starts next month; the glasses now live by the door.
- **57** Mahjong at Xiao Li's parents': Sam fills the fourth seat and wins his first hand.
  Remember: they play for fun, not money; the father is back home; Sam is now called 门槛精.
- **58** The manager marries on Saturday: Xiao Li explains the red envelope, the present, suit and tie.
  Remember: the bride is from Suzhou and was at university with the manager; Sam gives a thousand and French chocolates.
- **59** Dongtai Road antique market: the auntie bargains an old mirror down from 800 to 360 for Sam.
  Remember: Sam owns the mirror; the auntie bargains for everything from now on.
- **60** Xiao Li drags Sam to the gym; Sam gives up after two minutes of jumping; they plan a hike on Saturday.
  Remember: Sam is tall and used to play football and basketball; Xiao Li is short and thin and swims.
- **61** The grandson's maths homework the night before a test; Sam helps, the auntie scolds.
  Remember: the boy is good at English, weaker at maths, loses his things; the test went well (L63).
- **62** The auntie takes Sam to a 滑稽戏 at the Grand Theatre; he laughs all evening and misses only a line or two.
  Remember: Sam now follows a whole show in Shanghainese; they mean to go again.
- **63** (review) Canteen, Monday: the wedding told, the weekend planned. Remember: Sam's back still hurts from the gym.
- **64** Xiao Zhang moves to a fifth-floor flat with no lift; Sam and A-Qiang carry the wardrobe up.
  Remember: she lives alone on her floor; the bed came later; the stairs are a running joke.
- **65** Rush hour on line 2: an incident holds the trains; Sam and Xiao Li run from the station to a 9.00 meeting.
  Remember: Sam leaves at seven and is always early; Xiao Li is never on time and gets home late.
- **66** Sam and Xiao Zhang cycle along Suzhou Creek to the Huangpu; an old man fishing speaks with 仔.
  Remember: 仔 / 搭仔 / 为仔 are taught as older forms; the course keeps 了.
- **67** The upstairs family's cat, 宝贝 (white), escapes; Sam gets it down from a tree and is bitten.
  Remember: the upstairs girl calls Sam 爷叔; the window was left open by her mother.
- **68** Sam's two-week cough: the auntie takes him to her doctor of Chinese medicine; pear soup cures him.
  Remember: Sam works too late (L51); the auntie's pear soup.
- **69** Xiao Li's breakfast tour: the four guardians, savoury soy milk, pan-fried buns.
  Remember: Xiao Li's father eats his dough stick with rice porridge.
- **70** (review) Sam visits Xiao Zhang's flat; they plan a bike ride. Remember: Xiao Zhang lives far from Sam (by metro).
- **71** National Day plans: Hangzhou two days, then the Yellow Mountains three; Sam books, Xiao Zhang plans.
  Remember: Sam can't drive; A-Qiang has never been to Beijing and wants to go.
- **72** Hangzhou: West Lake, the pagoda, Dongpo pork, Longjing tea; clouds hide the moon.
  Remember: Xiao Zhang has been ten times; Sam wants to go back in spring (L77).
- **73** A rainy afternoon: the auntie on her childhood in a shikumen house (pavilion room, coal stove, chamber pots).
  Remember: she was born in the lane, is seventy, family of five in the 亭子间; she still has a marble.
- **74** December: the auntie has a padded jacket and trousers made by her tailor; Sam orders an overcoat, she gives him a scarf.
  Remember: the auntie wants "well made, crisp", not fashionable; Sam is tall.
- **75** Sam takes the wrong exit at People's Square; an old man directs him north to Nanjing Road by the compass.
  Remember: Sam says he is still learning; Shanghainese give directions by the compass.
- **76** Songjiang: Xiao Li's paternal grandmother 阿娘 (91, retired 30 years, three sons, one daughter married to a Suzhou man).
  Remember: she calls Sam 小马 (from his surname, Martin); Xiao Li's 外婆 (mother's side) is in Suzhou (L36).
- **77** (review) A café in mid-December with Xiao Zhang. Remember: next trip Beijing in spring, with A-Qiang.
