title: 礼拜天 白相 | A Sunday out
setting: Friday evening. Sam phones Xiao Li, a colleague who grew up in Hongkou.
--- lines
Sam: 小李[ɕiɔ34_li13] ， 礼拜天 侬 空 伐 ？ | Xiao Li, are you free on Sunday? | Little-Li, Sunday you free (question)?
小李: 空 个 。 做啥 ？ | Yes, I'm free. Why? | free (I am). do-what?
Sam: 阿拉 一道 去 豫园[ɦy13_ɦyø13] 白相 好 伐 ？ | Shall we go to Yuyuan together? | we together go Yuyuan play good (question)?
小李: 好个 ！ 几点钟 ？ | Sure! What time?
Sam: 早浪 十 点钟 ， 我 辣 地铁站 等 侬 。 | Ten in the morning. I'll wait for you at the metro station.
小李: 好个 。 阿拉 中饭 吃 小笼 ！ | OK. Let's have soup dumplings for lunch!
Sam: 灵 个 ！ 我 老 欢喜 吃 小笼 个 。 | Great! I love soup dumplings. | great (it is)! I very like eat soup-dumpling (really)
小李: 覅 忘记 带 钞票 。 | Don't forget to bring money.
Sam: 晓得 了 ， 礼拜天 见[ʨi34] ！ | Got it, see you Sunday! | know (now), Sunday see!
--- notes
1: 空 khon, said with the departing tone, is "free, not busy". 礼拜天 is Sunday: Shanghainese counts the week in 礼拜 (Mandarin 星期): 礼拜一 Monday … 礼拜六 Saturday.
2: 做啥 "do what?" is the usual way to ask "why? what for?". It is softer than 为啥 "why".
3: 阿拉 here includes the person you are talking to: "let's". 一道 is "together" (Mandarin 一起). 豫园 is the old garden and bazaar in the south of the city.
5: 早浪 is "in the morning". Time comes before the verb: 早浪十点钟 "(at) ten in the morning". 辣 + place again: 辣地铁站 "at the metro station".
7: 灵 lin is "great, works well": 灵个！ "great!". 欢喜 is "to like" (Mandarin 喜欢, the two characters in the other order).
8: 覅 "don't" again (lesson 3). 带 is "to bring", 钞票 "money".
9: 晓得了 "(I) know (now)" = "got it". 晓得 is "to know" (Mandarin 知道).
